Gesture Dictionary

One hand, eight controls.

Each landmark relationship — the distance and position between fingertips — is translated into a distinct system-level action. Customize settings inside Project.py.

☝️
Point
Index finger extended, others curled. Drives smooth, responsive cursor movement.
→ MOVE CURSOR
🤏
Single Pinch
Thumb and index fingertip pinch together. Simulates a standard single left click.
→ LEFT CLICK
⚡
Double Pinch
Two quick thumb-and-index pinches within 0.4 seconds triggers double click.
→ DOUBLE CLICK
👆
Right Click
Pinch thumb and middle finger together. Simulates a standard single right click.
→ RIGHT CLICK
🎯
Drag & Drop
Pinch thumb and index finger, then hold and move your hand to drag items.
→ DRAG & DROP
↕️
Scroll Mode
Extend all 4 fingers. Upper zone of active area scrolls up, lower zone scrolls down.
→ SCROLL UP / DOWN
📸
Screenshot
Pinch thumb and pinky finger (peace sign with pinky touch) to capture the screen.
→ TAKE SCREENSHOT
⌨️
Type Letter
Make a fist, then release specific fingers: Index (types A), Index+Middle (types B), Index+Middle+Ring (types C).
→ TYPE KEYBOARD A/B/C
